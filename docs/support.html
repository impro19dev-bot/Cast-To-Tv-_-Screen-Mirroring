Troubleshooting

  1. Keep phone and TV on the same Wi‑Fi (avoid guest networks / AP isolation).
  2. Temporarily disable VPN.
  3. Enable AirPlay / screen sharing on the TV or streaming box.
  4. Restart TV and router, then reopen the route picker.
  5. For full screen mirroring: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → choose display → Stop Mirroring when done.

Permissions

Check Settings → Privacy for Photos, Local Network, and Screen Recording access for this app if casting or the Wi‑Fi viewer fails.

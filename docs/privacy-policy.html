Privacy Policy

Cast To Tv _ Screen Mirroring

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Overview

This independent app helps you cast media and follow AirPlay / screen-mirroring guidance. We do not sell personal data and we do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

On-device preferences

Language, appearance, and haptic settings are stored locally with Shared Preferences / UserDefaults. We do not sync these to a remote profile service.

Media access

Photos and videos you select stay under your control. They are used to preview and cast via system AirPlay APIs. We do not upload your library to our servers.

Permissions

Local Network — discover AirPlay devices and optionally serve a LAN Wi‑Fi viewer.
Photos — pick images/videos.
Microphone / Screen Recording — may be requested by iOS when starting the optional Wi‑Fi viewer.

Network

The in-app browser loads URLs you enter. The optional Wi‑Fi viewer serves JPEG frames only on your local network. We do not operate a backend that receives your screen content or browsing history.
